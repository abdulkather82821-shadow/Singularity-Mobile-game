How to Play

Ajees must survive three stages of the dimensional invasion, gather rift energy, and face Katleen in her own realm. Move in all four directions across the arena, jump over danger, and keep your combo alive.

Desktop Controls

AD Move left / right
WS Move up / down (depth)
Space Jump / evade
J Melee strike
K Energy shot
L Awakened burst (special)
H Mend (restore health)
Shift Dash (brief invulnerability)
Esc Pause / resume

Arrow keys work for movement as well. Hold J or K to keep attacking.

Mobile Controls

Use the virtual stick on the left to move in any direction across the arena. On the right: STRIKE, SHOT, SPECIAL, DASH and JUMP. Tap ❚❚ to pause. Touch controls appear automatically on touch devices and can be forced in Settings.

Combat Rules

  • Melee hits in a short arc in front of Ajees. Each swing can damage an enemy only once.
  • Energy shots cost a little energy. Energy regenerates steadily and rift orbs refill it.
  • Special detonates a shockwave around Ajees and costs a large chunk of energy. It unlocks after the awakening.
  • Dash grants brief invulnerability and passes through danger. Watch its cooldown.
  • Taking damage breaks your combo. Chaining hits raises your score multiplier up to x4.
  • Purple ground rings are telegraphed rift eruptions — leave the ring before it detonates.

Progression & Choices

  • Level up by felling wraiths and gathering rift energy. Each level lets you pick a permanent upgrade (power, energy, healing, dash, vitality, awakened core).
  • Mend (H / MEND) spends energy to restore health — unlocked by choosing the Vessel Mend path at the first crossroads.
  • Choices matter. Story crossroads change your skills and the ending you reach. Between stages you may stand with your allies or walk the rift alone.
  • Your path is recorded on the victory screen — replay to see the other endings.
Survive the campus, hold the rift, and reach Katleen with enough health to finish the fight. Progress and best scores are saved on this device when storage is available.
